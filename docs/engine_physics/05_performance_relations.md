# 5. Performance relations

[Index](README.md) · Physics reference

**Indicated mean effective pressure (IMEP):** IMEP = W_i / V_d, where W_i is the indicated work per cycle [D].

**Brake mean effective pressure (BMEP):** BMEP = W_b / V_d, where W_b is the brake work per cycle [D].

**Torque from BMEP (four-stroke):** T = BMEP · V_d / (4π) [D].

**Power from BMEP (four-stroke):** P = BMEP · V_d · N / 120 [D].

**Friction mean effective pressure:** FMEP = IMEP − BMEP [D].

**Mechanical efficiency:** η_m = BMEP / IMEP = 1 − FMEP / IMEP [D].

**Volumetric efficiency (four-stroke):** η_v = ṁ_a / (ρ_a,0 · V_d · N / 120), where ρ_a,0 is the density of the air at intake reference conditions [D].

**Brake thermal efficiency:** η_bt = P / (ṁ_f · LHV) [D].

**Brake-specific fuel consumption:** bsfc = ṁ_f / P [D].

**Typical values [T]:**
- Mechanical efficiency at full load: 0.80–0.90.
- Brake thermal efficiency, spark-ignition engines at best torque: 0.25–0.35.
- Volumetric efficiency at full load: 0.7–0.9 depending on speed and tuning.
