# 12. Measurement methods

[Index](README.md) · Physics reference

- **Dial-gauge lift check:** mount the cam on V-blocks, read lift every 5° of rotation. Gives lift profile, base circle, duration at a stated lift [T, common practice].
- **Degree-wheel timing:** read opening and closing angles at a stated lift, usually 1 mm [T, common practice].
- **Motoring test or coast-down:** separates friction from combustion losses [T, common practice].
- **Wideband lambda:** place the sensor where reversion from exhaust gas will not distort the reading [T].
- **Cylinder-pressure trace:** gives IMEP, heat release, burn duration and CA50 [T, common practice].
- **GPS speed:** the reference for road-speed checks; speedometers read high [T, common experience, not measured here].
