# Honda V3R 900 E-Compressor research

Research checked 8 November 2026. This motorcycle is a planned production model; final specifications have not been published.

## Confirmed by Honda

- Honda introduced a water-cooled 75-degree V3 with an electrically driven intake compressor at EICMA 2024. The compressor controls intake compression independently of engine speed; Honda says the design does not need an intercooler. [Honda 2024](https://global.honda/en/newsroom/news/2024/c241105ceng.html)
- The V3R 900 prototype was shown at EICMA 2025, with approximately 900 cc displacement and an asymmetric design. [Honda 2025](https://global.honda/en/newsroom/news/2025/c251104aeng.html)
- At EICMA 2026 Honda showed the planned production model, with asymmetric bodywork, titanium fuel tank, exhaust pipes and muffler, and enhanced RoadSync. [Honda 2026](https://global.honda/en/newsroom/news/2026/2261103aeng.html)
- Honda's [prototype video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdwQHK-IiRc) and [360-degree video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TtqB9OnlANk) provide appearance references; neither establishes a calibrated engine-sound reference.

## Other reporting and simulator assumptions

Published reporting describes two cylinders facing forward and one rearward. [Motorcycles News](https://www.motorcycles.news/en/honda-v3r-900-e-compressor-eicma-2026-production-model/). A Honda dealer reports that the prototype has a separate compressor battery, but its voltage and capacity are undisclosed. [Honda Motorrad Zürich](https://honda-motorrad-zuerich.ch/en/honda-v3r900-e-compressor/)

Honda has not published bore, stroke, firing order, crankpin arrangement, compression ratio, maximum boost, compressor power or battery capacity, redline, horsepower, torque, weight, dimensions, gearbox ratios, or a validated engine sound. The preset's values, electric budget, body panels and synthesized compressor whine are adjustable simulator estimates, not Honda specifications. The simulated electrical budget is a simple energy approximation, not a prediction of the production charging system. Geometry can be changed without implying a particular Honda crankshaft design.
