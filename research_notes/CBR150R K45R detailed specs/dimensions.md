# Honda CBR150R K45R (2021-2026) - Dimensions and Component Masses

Scope: K45R only. Figures from K45N/K45G/K45A/older generations are flagged where they appear in sources. No primary Honda (AHM) spec sheet or owner's manual was retrieved. All figures below are from secondary sources and should be treated as unverified until checked against AHM material.

## Overall dimensions (length x width x height)

### Takeaway
Length and wheelbase agree across sources. Width and height disagree (height 1,038 mm vs 1,077 mm vs 1,080 mm), which likely reflects a generation or measurement difference, not a single true value.

### Cited Findings
- Length 1,983 mm, wheelbase 1,310 mm; 2021 model width 700 mm, height 1,077 mm (table column for 2021-present) - [Wikipedia Honda CBR150R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_CBR150R) (table fetched; column mapping partly unclear for length/wheelbase)
- 1,983 x 700 x 1,080 mm, wheelbase 1,310 mm, for the 2021 All New model - [Autofun](https://www.autofun.co.id/berita-motor/Simak-Spesifikasi-All-New-Honda-CBR150R-2021-Kena-Update-Fitur-dan-Desain-Makin-Agresif-22412) (page returned HTTP 403 on fetch; figure taken from search snippet, not verified in full text)
- 1,983 x 700 x 1,080 mm, wheelbase 1,310 mm, from a review of the 2021 generation - [Zigwheels](https://www.zigwheels.co.id/motor-baru/honda/cbr150r/) (fetched, reviewed text)
- 1,983 x 694 x 1,038 mm (older generation per Cermati, dated) - [Cermati](https://www.cermati.com/kredit-motor/honda-all-new-cbr-150r); contradicted by Autofun's note that 2021 dimensions are 1,983 x 694 x 1,077 mm for the previous generation
- Wheelbase 1,310 mm and length 1,983 mm also listed on a generic spec page - [91wheels](https://91wheels.com/bikes/honda/cbr150r/specifications) (search snippet; generation not stated)

### Inferences
- Length (1,983 mm) and wheelbase (1,310 mm) are consistent across at least three sources. Treat as likely correct for K45R, still unverified against AHM.
- Width 700 mm is more likely K45R than 694 mm, which appears to be the prior generation (K45G/N).
- Height is the least certain: 1,077 mm and 1,080 mm are close, 1,038 mm appears to be an older generation.

### Gaps
- No AHM or dealer page for K45R was retrieved with a readable spec table. hondacengkareng.com lists K45R under its model menu but its CBR150R page shows no figures (fetched).
- astra-honda.com and hondamandalajayaabadi.com were not reached in this session.

## Seat height, ground clearance

### Takeaway
Seat height for K45R is most likely 782 mm. Ground clearance is contested (160 mm, 166 mm, 151 mm).

### Cited Findings
- Seat height 782 mm, ground clearance 160 mm, height 1,077 mm (2021-present column) - [Wikipedia Honda CBR150R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_CBR150R) (fetched)
- Seat height 782 mm, ground clearance 160 mm - [Oto.com](https://www.oto.com/bandingkan-motor/honda-cbr150r-and-suzuki-gsx-s150) (search snippet only)
- Seat height 787 mm, ground clearance 166 mm - [Cermati](https://www.cermati.com/kredit-motor/honda-all-new-cbr-150r) (search snippet; contradicted by Wikipedia and Oto.com for K45R, and likely an older generation)
- Ground clearance 166 mm (FAQ, no year/variant) - [Autofun](https://www.autofun.co.id/berita-motor/segala-pertanyaan-yang-perlu-diketahui-soal-all-new-honda-cbr150r-2021-22703) (fetched via review FAQ; generation not stated)
- Seat height 788 mm, ground clearance 151 mm - a Philippine spec sheet for a later model (search snippet; treat as unverified for K45R)

### Inferences
- Ground clearance 160 mm is the best-supported K45R figure (Wikipedia 2021 column and Oto.com agree). 166 mm matches the K45G/N generation.

### Gaps
- No official Honda figure for ground clearance was retrieved.

## Weights

### Takeaway
Curb weight for K45R is contested: 137 kg (non-ABS) / 139 kg (ABS) per Autofun and Zigwheels, 135 kg per Cermati and other older listings, and 151 kg per the Wikipedia 2021 column. No dry weight or axle split was found.

### Cited Findings
- Curb weight 137 kg non-ABS, 139 kg ABS (2021 generation) - [Autofun](https://www.autofun.co.id/berita-motor/Simak-Spesifikasi-All-New-Honda-CBR150R-2021-Kena-Update-Fitur-dan-Desain-Makin-Agresif-22412) (search snippet); also [Zigwheels](https://www.zigwheels.co.id/motor-baru/honda/cbr150r/) (fetched review text). The ABS difference is attributed to the ABS unit and ESS module.
- Curb weight 151 kg for 2021 - [Wikipedia Honda CBR150R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_CBR150R) (from table text); contradicted by Autofun/Zigwheels 137/139 kg
- Wet weight (with oil and fuel) 135 kg - [Cermati](https://www.cermati.com/kredit-motor/honda-all-new-cbr-150r) (older listing); contradicted by 137/139 kg above
- Curb weight 135 kg - a generic spec listing (search snippet, generation not stated)

### Inferences
- Likely K45R curb weight is 137 kg (non-ABS) and 139 kg (ABS), since two 2021-specific sources agree and the 151 kg figure appears to be a table mapping or a different measurement basis (not confirmed).
- The 2 kg ABS difference is plausible but its source is a secondary article.

### Gaps
- Dry weight not published in any source found.
- Front/rear axle split not published.
- Weight measurement standard (kerb with fluids, tank full or partial) not stated in any source.

## Fuel tank

### Takeaway
Tank capacity 12 L is consistently reported for the K45R generation.

### Cited Findings
- 12 litres - [Zigwheels](https://www.zigwheels.co.id/motor-baru/honda/cbr150r/) (fetched review text, no year/variant)
- 12 L (3.1 US gal) - [Wheeltek](https://wheeltek.com.ph/?p=4125) and [Top Gear PH](https://www.topgear.com.ph/index.php/buyers-guide/motorcycles/honda/cbr150r) (search snippets; the Top Gear PH page is for the 2019 model, a K45N-era spec)
- Wikipedia table lists fuel capacity for K45R column as unclear; 12 L is the likely candidate - [Wikipedia Honda CBR150R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_CBR150R)

### Gaps
- Fuel-cap location is not described in any fetched source. Not verified.
