# CBR150R K45R (2021-2026) Drivetrain and Chassis

Scope: Honda CBR150R K45R, 2021 to present generation. The K45N/K45G (2016-present) and K45A (2014-2016) figures are flagged where they appear. The 2004 CBR150R gear ratios are not used.

Overall status: the K45R gear ratios, primary reduction, final drive ratio, sprocket teeth, chain link count and dry weight could NOT be verified from any source reached. Tyre sizes and curb/wet weight are verified for the K45R with some contradictions. No official Honda (AHM) or Astra Honda spec page or parts catalogue was reachable: the Astra Honda, Honda Indonesia, hondacengkareng and hondamandalajayaabadi pages either were not returned by search or returned HTTP 403 (autofun.co.id spec page, Wikipedia revision fetch).

## Gearbox ratios (1st to 6th) and primary reduction

### Takeaway
No verified K45R gear ratios or primary reduction ratio were found. The only numeric gear ratios found belong to older CBR150R generations and must not be used for the K45R.

### Cited Findings
- K45R transmission is a 6-speed constant-mesh manual, with no ratio values given — [Wikipedia, Honda CBR150R, Specifications table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_CBR150R)
- K45R (2021 Repsol edition listing) reads "6 Speed" with no ratios — [motorcyclespecs.co.za, CBR150R rep 21](https://www.motorcyclespecs.co.za/model/Honda/honda_cbr150r_rep_21.html)
- 2021 K45R carries an assist/slipper clutch; the spec-news source gives no ratios — [AutoFun, All New CBR150R 2021](https://www.autofun.co.id/berita-motor/Simak-Spesifikasi-All-New-Honda-CBR150R-2021-Kena-Update-Fitur-dan-Desain-Makin-Agresif-22412)
- Older-generation 6-speed set (1st 3.038, 2nd 1.941, 3rd 1.500, 4th 1.227, 5th 1.041, 6th 0.923) appears on a 2006-2007 and 2019-2020 listing. NOT K45R — these are the older generation, not valid for K45R. Source: [motorcyclespecs.co.za, CBR150R 06](https://www.motorcyclespecs.co.za/model/Honda/honda_cbr150r_06.html) (as reported by search snippet; the page itself was not fetched, so treat as unverified even for the older bike).
- Contradiction on an older (2010) generation: 1st gear 3.083, major reduction 3.260, final reduction 2.933 — [Bikes Republic, 2010 CBR150R](https://www.bikesrepublic.com/bm/2010-honda-cbr150r-technical-specification). Pre-K45R, NOT valid for K45R. Also conflicts with the 3.038 first-gear figure above, which is itself an unresolved older-generation conflict.

### Inferences
- The K45R is a 6-speed manual with a wet multi-plate clutch (see clutch section) and is not expected to share the 2010 or 2006 ratio sets. That is an inference from generation differences, not a verified statement.

### Gaps
- K45R gear ratios 1st through 6th: not found. Needs the Honda K45R service manual, a Honda parts catalogue (gear tooth counts), or a verified Honda Indonesia/Astra Honda spec sheet.
- K45R primary reduction ratio: not found in any source reached.
- No source gave gear tooth counts for any CBR150R generation.

## Official curb and dry weights

### Takeaway
K45R curb (ready-to-ride) weight is reported as 137 kg (2021-2022) and 138 kg (2023), with an outlier of 151 kg on Wikipedia and 157 kg claimed by a Bangladesh listing. No K45R dry weight was verified.

### Cited Findings
- K45R wet weight "137 kg / 302 lbs" (2021 Repsol edition listing) — [motorcyclespecs.co.za, CBR150R rep 21](https://www.motorcyclespecs.co.za/model/Honda/honda_cbr150r_rep_21.html)
- K45R curb weight 137 kg (2022) and 138 kg (2023), "Leergewicht fahrfertig" — [motorradundreisen.de, CBR150R data](https://www.motorradundreisen.de/motorraddatenbank/honda/cbr-150r-12361.html)
- K45R curb weight 151 kg (Wikipedia, 2021-present history section) — [Wikipedia, Honda CBR150R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_CBR150R); contradicted by [motorcyclespecs.co.za, rep 21](https://www.motorcyclespecs.co.za/model/Honda/honda_cbr150r_rep_21.html) (137 kg) and [motorradundreisen.de](https://www.motorradundreisen.de/motorraddatenbank/honda/cbr-150r-12361.html) (137/138 kg)
- Bangladesh listing: 157 kg "claimed by Honda" (market and year not stated) — [Pakwheels/BikeBD search snippet](https://bikebd.com/price/honda-cbr-150r/color); unverified, contradicted by the figures above
- Indian listing for 2022-2024: kerb weight 138 kg — search snippet only, unverified page
- Pre-K45R (2013-2017) dry weight 138 kg — [motorradundreisen.de](https://www.motorradundreisen.de/motorraddatenbank/honda/cbr-150r-12361.html); 2007 dry weight 115 kg. These are older generations; they are NOT K45R.

### Inferences
- Wet (fuel-included) and curb (fuel and oil included) figures are the same family of measurement for these sources, so 137-138 kg is the most consistent K45R figure. The 151 kg Wikipedia figure looks like a different measurement basis or market. Dry weight is likely lower than 137 kg, but no number was found.

### Gaps
- K45R dry weight: not found in any source reached.
- Honda's official measurement standard (dry vs curb vs wet) for the K45R is not confirmed.
- The 151 kg and 157 kg figures could not be reconciled with 137-138 kg.

## Front and rear tyre sizes (K45R)

### Takeaway
K45R front is 100/80-17 and rear is 130/70-17. Verified by multiple sources, with no contradiction found.

### Cited Findings
- Front 100/80-17, rear 130/70-17 — [motorcyclespecs.co.za, CBR150R rep 21](https://www.motorcyclespecs.co.za/model/Honda/honda_cbr150r_rep_21.html) (2021 listing)
- Front 100/80-17 and rear 130/70-17 for 2022 and 2023 entries — [motorradundreisen.de](https://www.motorradundreisen.de/motorraddatenbank/honda/cbr-150r-12361.html)
- Front 100/80-17 and rear 130/70-17 — [Wikipedia, Honda CBR150R, K45R column](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_CBR150R) (inferred from two-value row; column mapping not labelled)
- Philippines spec sheet: 100/80-17 M/C 52P (Tubeless) front, 130/70-17 M/C 62P (Tubeless) rear — [wheeltek.com.ph](https://wheeltek.com.ph/vehicles/cbr150r/) (generation not confirmed as K45R; load index and tubeless marking are unverified for K45R)

### Inferences
- Tyre sizes are consistent across generations, so these are likely unchanged for K45R. Load index and speed rating come only from one Philippine source.

### Gaps
- Official Honda tyre spec for K45R (load index, speed rating): not confirmed by Honda source.

## Additional chassis figures (verified or flagged)

### Wheelbase
- K45R wheelbase 1310 mm / 51.5 in — [motorcyclespecs.co.za, CBR150R rep 21](https://www.motorcyclespecs.co.za/model/Honda/honda_cbr150r_rep_21.html); also [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_CBR150R) (the K45R column row has four values across five columns, so mapping is inferred; the 2021 listing is the cleaner source).
- Older generation 1305 mm (2013-2015): not K45R, from search snippet only.

### Seat height
- K45R 782 mm / 30.8 in — [motorcyclespecs.co.za, rep 21](https://www.motorcyclespecs.co.za/model/Honda/honda_cbr150r_rep_21.html); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_CBR150R) (K45R column, from the last column).
- CONTRADICTION: 787 mm (Wikipedia, K45N/K45G 2016-present column) and 788 mm (motorradundreisen.de 2022 and 2023 entries, which the page implicitly ties to K45R). Source at [motorradundreisen.de](https://www.motorradundreisen.de/motorraddatenbank/honda/cbr-150r-12361.html). Not resolved: 782 vs 787 vs 788 mm.
- Note: 787 mm is the K45N/K45G figure per Wikipedia, not K45R. Flagged.

### Ground clearance
- K45R 160 mm / 6.3 in — [motorcyclespecs.co.za, rep 21](https://www.motorcyclespecs.co.za/model/Honda/honda_cbr150r_rep_21.html); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_CBR150R).
- K45N/K45G figure: 166 mm (Wikipedia). Flagged, not K45R.
- Malaysia-market listing 151 mm (search snippet). Different market, not K45R.

### Fuel tank capacity
- K45R 12 L / 3.1 US gal — [motorcyclespecs.co.za, rep 21](https://www.motorcyclespecs.co.za/model/Honda/honda_cbr150r_rep_21.html); [motorradundreisen.de, 2022 and 2023](https://www.motorradundreisen.de/motorraddatenbank/honda/cbr-150r-12361.html); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_CBR150R) (last value in K45R row).
- CONTRADICTION: 13.1 L (Indonesian detik spec, older generation per search snippet, [detik](https://oto.detik.com/motor/d-1672080/honda-konsumsi-bensin-cbr150r-1-35-6)); 13 L (Pakwheels and an Indian listing, 2022-2024 per snippet). Unresolved for K45R.

### Brakes and ABS
- K45R front and rear: single hydraulic wave disc, 220 mm (motorcyclespecs.co.za rep 21; motorradundreisen.de).
- ABS: 2-channel ABS listed as optional on the 2021 Repsol edition (motorcyclespecs.co.za); 2023 entry lists ABS on motorradundreisen.de. Standard vs optional for the base K45R is unresolved.

### Clutch
- Wet multi-plate with assist/slipper function: [motorcyclespecs.co.za, rep 21](https://www.motorcyclespecs.co.za/model/Honda/honda_cbr150r_rep_21.html).
- Anti-hopping clutch listed for 2022 and 2023 on [motorradundreisen.de](https://www.motorradundreisen.de/motorraddatenbank/honda/cbr-150r-12361.html).
- Clutch plate count, clutch cable type and spring specs: not found.

### Frame and suspension
- Diamond truss frame (motorcyclespecs.co.za rep 21). Rake 91 mm (3.6 in). Trail value garbled on page ("25", unit unclear).
- Front upside-down telescopic fork and dual rear shocks listed for 2022 and 2023 (motorradundreisen.de).
- Frame material "Diamond, steel" listed for older generations (2015-2017); K45R frame material not confirmed.

## Source quality notes
- Wikipedia's K45R column was fetched through a cached revision. Its own table is flagged "better source needed."
- motorcyclespecs.co.za pages are aggregator spec databases, not Honda sources. Their 2021 page is the Repsol MotoGP edition; its drivetrain and chassis values may match the base bike, but this was not confirmed. Its description says "50cc" while its table says 149.4 cc, which suggests a data-entry error on that page.
- motorradundreisen.de has an editorial-review notice and inconsistent engine text (single-cylinder vs "Zweizylinder" in 2022 and 2023 entries).
- Several search-snippet-only results (Bangladesh, Indian, Philippine, detik) were not fetched in full and are marked as such.
- No Honda (AHM), Astra Honda, hondacengkareng.com or hondamandalajayaabadi.com page was successfully retrieved.
